## 3\. Information We Collect

We collect information in two primary ways:

### a) Information you provide directly

- **Beta Waitlist Signups:** When you join our beta waitlist, we collect your name and email address.  
- **Contact Forms:** When you reach out through any contact form on our site, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.  
- **Other Voluntary Submissions:** Any additional information you choose to provide to us, such as social media handles, artist profile details, or feedback.

### b) Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

- IP address and approximate location (city/region level)  
- Browser type and version  
- Device type and operating system  
- Pages you visit and time spent on each  
- Referring website or source  
- Date and time of access

This data is collected through cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies (see Section 4).

## 4\. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate our website, understand how it's used, and deliver relevant advertising. The categories of cookies we use include:

- **Essential cookies:** Required for the website to function properly (e.g., session management, security).  
- **Analytics cookies:** Help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it.  
- **Advertising and marketing cookies:** Used to deliver relevant ads to you on platforms like Facebook and Instagram, measure the effectiveness of those ads, and build audiences for future campaigns.

By using our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this Policy. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features of our site may not work properly if you do.

## 5\. Meta Pixel and Third-Party Advertising

Our website uses the **Meta Pixel**, a tracking tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (the parent company of Facebook and Instagram). The Meta Pixel collects information including:

- The pages you visit on our site  
- The actions you take (such as joining the beta waitlist)  
- A hashed (one-way encrypted) version of your email address and name, used to help Meta match you with your Facebook or Instagram account  
- Browser, device, and session data

This information is used to:

- Show you relevant advertising on Facebook, Instagram, and other Meta-owned platforms  
- Measure the performance of our ad campaigns  
- Build custom and lookalike audiences for future marketing

Meta processes this data in accordance with its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy). You can manage your ad preferences or opt out of personalized advertising through your [Meta ad settings](https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences) or through industry tools such as the [Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada opt-out page](https://youradchoices.ca/).

We may add additional advertising or analytics partners in the future and will update this Policy accordingly.

## 8\. How We Share Your Information

**We do not sell your personal information.** We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** With trusted third parties who perform services on our behalf, such as email delivery, website hosting, customer support, and analytics. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.  
- **Advertising Partners:** With platforms like Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for advertising purposes, as described in Section 5\.  
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, court order, or government request, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.  
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of all or part of our business, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. You will be notified of any such change.

We use commercially reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. ## 10\. Your Rights

Depending on where you reside, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you  
- **Correction:** Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information  
- **Deletion:** Request that we delete your personal information (subject to legal exceptions)  
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw your consent to our processing of your information at any time  
- **Object or Restrict:** Object to or restrict certain types of processing  
- **Data Portability:** Request a copy of your information in a portable format  
- **File a Complaint:** File a complaint with a privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at [priv.gc.ca](https://priv.gc.ca/)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at \[[hello@lisnin.io](mailto:hello@lisnin.io)\]. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.
